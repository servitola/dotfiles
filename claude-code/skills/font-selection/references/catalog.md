# Catalog: the 40 faces, and what you can actually use instead

Every face the poster can land on, plus what it costs and the closest thing that is free and
web-embeddable. "Free" here means an open licence (SIL OFL / Apache) — safe to self-host, bundle
in an app, and subset. Retail desktop licences usually cover none of that.

Substitutes match *role and voice*, not outlines. Say what changes when you propose one.

## Old-style and transitional serifs

| Face | Voice | Good at | Where to get | Free substitute |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Garamond | warm, low-contrast, small on the body | book text, long reading | Adobe Fonts (Garamond Premier), Monotype | **EB Garamond** — very close, has Cyrillic |
| Sabon | Garamond with a bigger x-height | book text that must hold at small sizes | Linotype/Monotype | EB Garamond at a size up, or Cormorant Garamond |
| Caslon | irregular, friendly, unmistakably English | the safe default when nothing else fits | Adobe Fonts (Adobe Caslon) | **Libre Caslon Text** |
| Minion | neutral, generous, never in the way | book and report text, huge language coverage | included with Adobe CC | Crimson Pro |
| Baskerville | crisp, upright, more contrast than a Garamond | literary books, formal print | macOS bundles Baskerville; Monotype | **Libre Baskerville** (built for screens) |
| Palatino | broad calligraphic strokes, Italian Renaissance | invitations, menus, warm print text | bundled with macOS and Office | **URW Palladio (P052)** — a true clone |
| Joanna | narrow, slab-ish serifs, quietly odd | books with a designed feel | Monotype | Vollkorn |
| FF Scala | humanist, sturdy, calm | books, catalogues, cultural print | Monotype | Alegreya |

## Didones and high-contrast display serifs

| Face | Voice | Good at | Where to get | Free substitute |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bodoni | vertical stress, hairline serifs | fashion, invitations, large sizes | many foundries; Adobe Fonts | **Libre Bodoni**, or Playfair Display for display |
| Didot | thinner still, colder | luxury and fashion covers | macOS bundles Didot; Linotype | Playfair Display, Prata |
| Walbaum | German didone, more readable than Didot | book and magazine text that still wants contrast | Monotype (Walbaum 2018) | Playfair Display at text weights |

Hairline serifs vanish on screen and in cheap print. Keep didones above ~24 px / 18 pt, or move
to an optical text cut.

## Geometric sans

| Face | Voice | Good at | Where to get | Free substitute |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Futura | circles and triangles, Bauhaus | logos, luxury, film titles | many foundries | **Jost\*** — the closest free geometric (the asterisk is part of its name) |
| Metro | geometric with humanist warmth | headlines with a softer edge | Linotype (Metro Nova) | Poppins, Jost\* |

Geometric sans have low legibility in long text — the round `o` and single-storey `a` slow
reading. Use them for headlines and set body text in something with more variation.

## Scripts and handwriting

| Face | Voice | Good at | Where to get | Free substitute |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zapfino | extravagant calligraphy, huge swashes | one line on an invitation | bundled with macOS; Linotype | Great Vibes, Tangerine |
| FF Erikrighthand | casual real handwriting | informal notes, personal invitations | Monotype | Caveat, Shantell Sans |

Scripts break in all caps and lose their point below ~24 pt. One line, generous size, no
letter-spacing.

## What the 2010 poster is missing

The chart predates the screen-first era. For app, web, and product work these belong in the
candidate set:

- **Inter** — the default UI grotesque: tall x-height, tabular figures, variable, wide Cyrillic.
- **IBM Plex Sans / Serif / Mono** — a complete open superfamily with a coherent voice.
- **Geist / Geist Mono** — contemporary product-UI grotesque, variable.
- **JetBrains Mono** — code face with tall lowercase and clear `1lI` / `0O`.
- **Instrument Serif, Fraunces, Newsreader** — free display and editorial serifs with real character.
- **Manrope, Onest, Golos Text** — modern geometric-humanist sans with strong Cyrillic.

## Cyrillic coverage

Most of the poster's faces ship Latin-only or charge extra for Cyrillic. Verified Cyrillic in the
free substitutes above: EB Garamond, Inter, Fira Sans, Montserrat, Playfair Display, Jost\*,
IBM Plex (Sans/Serif/Mono), Literata, Source Sans 3, Manrope, Golos Text, PT Sans/PT Serif,
Alegreya (Sans and Serif). Check the rest on the foundry page before promising Russian text.
